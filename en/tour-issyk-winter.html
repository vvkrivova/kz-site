Winter route:
Issyk-Kul

A journey to the snowy peaks of the Tien Shan: winter landscapes of Issyk-Kul, skiing in Karakol, red rocks and hot springs. The trip starts in Bishkek and goes through Cholpon-Ata, Karakol, Ak-Suu, Jeti-Oguz and Ala-Archa — we’ve marked the main stops on the map
