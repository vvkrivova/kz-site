Never driven

Can I do it?

Before handover we’ll show you the controls and equipment, and drive the first kilometres together. A motorhome is bigger than a regular car, so what matters most is getting used to its size calmly, not previous motorhome experience.

02

What if the vehicle breaks down?

You won’t be left alone with the problem. We agree in advance what we do in case of a fault, when we can fix it on the spot and when the vehicle can be replaced

03

What if the weather turns bad or a road becomes impassable?

If conditions on a section change, we help adjust the route and choose a safe option from those available.

That’s why our routes note seasonality and the sections that depend on the weather.
