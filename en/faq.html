Any questions?

Short answers to what people usually want to know before booking

We’re here
01

Do I need special experience to drive a motorhome?

No special motorhome experience is needed. Before the trip we’ll introduce you to the vehicle, explain how it handles and show you how to use the equipment and the living module.

For self-drive rental, the driver must be over 25, have at least 5 years of driving experience and hold a valid licence of the appropriate category.

02

Can I travel on my own route?

Yes! You can choose one of the ready-made Tian Way routes or suggest your own destination.

We’ll help you check road access for your motorhome, take seasonal restrictions into account, find suitable campsites and plan your stops. Some mountain and hard-to-reach sections need to be agreed in advance.

03

What does the motorhome rental include?

You get a travel-ready motorhome with a living module and equipment according to the chosen model’s specification.

The price also includes 250 km of mileage per rental day. Unused mileage carries over for the whole trip.

Extra camping gear can be rented separately. The exact list of included services and equipment is shown on each motorhome’s card.

See gear rental
04

How do I book a motorhome and what deposit is required?

To confirm a booking, a 30% prepayment of the rental cost is required.

The remaining 70% is paid before the trip starts. There is also a refundable $500 deposit, paid separately and returned after the motorhome’s condition and equipment are checked according to the contract.

See motorhomes
05

How is a guided trip different from a regular rental?

With a self-drive rental you travel at your own pace, choose where to stop and plan your days as you like.

A Tian Way trip is a ready-made programme with a route, sights, activities and organisational support. Depending on the programme, we also arrange transport, accommodation and meals.

What’s included is listed in the description of each trip.
